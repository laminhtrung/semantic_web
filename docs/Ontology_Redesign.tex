\section{\texorpdfstring{D\allowbreak{}Bpedia-\allowbreak{}based Movie
Ontology: Evidence\allowbreak{}-\allowbreak{}driven
Design}{ Movie Ontology:  Design}}\label{dbpedia-based-movie-ontology-evidence-driven-design}

This design is grounded in the existing crawled dataset. Domain facts
are not invented. The native build, application and query modes share
these version-\allowbreak{}three definitions and canonical exports.

\subsection{1. Findings and semantic
decisions}\label{findings-and-semantic-decisions}

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  Reuse dbo:Film, Person, Actor, Writer,
  Movie\allowbreak{}Dir\allowbreak{}ector,
  Screen\allowbreak{}Wr\allowbreak{}iter, Producer, Company,
  Organisa\allowbreak{}tion, Award, MovieGenre, Genre, Country,
  Language, Work, Artist and Agent. Actor follows the original
  dbo:starring range; no
  equivale\allowbreak{}nt-\allowbreak{}class\allowbreak{} override of
  dbo:Actor.
\item
  Reuse dbo:\allowbreak{}dire\allowbreak{}ctor, starring, writer,
  producer, producti\allowbreak{}on\allowbreak{}Compan\allowbreak{}y,
  award, genre, country, language and runtime. Preserve reference
  domain/\allowbreak{}r\allowbreak{}ange:\allowbreak{} starring ranges
  over Actor, producer over Agent,
  producti\allowbreak{}on\allowbreak{}Compan\allowbreak{}y over Company.
\item
  dbo:\allowbreak{}\allowbreak{}Movi\allowbreak{}e\allowbreak{}Directo\allowbreak{}r,
  ScreenWriter and Producer are reused for
  occupati\allowbreak{}on-\allowbreak{}level\allowbreak{} inferences
  from the correspo\allowbreak{}nding credits. One-way subclass axioms
  suffice; their global definitions are not overridden. - dbo:runtime is
  seconds (xsd:\allowbreak{}dou\allowbreak{}ble); Inception has 8880
  seconds, converted from the crawled 148 minutes. Titles reuse
  rdfs:label (annotation property, not a custom datatype property).
\item
  P577 has varying precision. Keep a documented local releaseYear
  summary instead of inventing a full
  dbo:\allowbreak{}rele\allowbreak{}ase\allowbreak{}Date.\allowbreak{}
  Budget, gross, IMDb, distributors and ratings are excluded because
  they are not in the collected field set.
\item
  Remove unsupported
  Document\allowbreak{}ary/\allowbreak{}\allowbreak{}Fict\allowbreak{}ion
  hierarchy and FeatureFilm assertions: Wikidata Q11424 means film and
  does not justify feature-film
  classifi\allowbreak{}cation.\allowbreak{} Award labels alone do not
  justify treating every unrecognized award as a film award.
\item
  ActionGenre and DramaGenre are transparent
  label-\allowbreak{}no\allowbreak{}rmalizat\allowbreak{}ion buckets,
  not source-\allowbreak{}p\allowbreak{}rovided OWL classes. The
  author-\allowbreak{}s\allowbreak{}pecified\allowbreak{}
  classifi\allowbreak{}cation rule is separate from the crawled facts;
  fiction/\allowbreak{}non-\allowbreak{}fict\allowbreak{}ion is not
  guessed.
\item
  Contribution and four role individuals represent
  P57/\allowbreak{}\allowbreak{}P161\allowbreak{}/\allowbreak{}\allowbreak{}P58/\allowbreak{}\allowbreak{}P16\allowbreak{}2
  credits. Each record has exactly one person, film and role. Missing
  values remain an open-world validation issue, not automatic
  inconsis\allowbreak{}tency.\allowbreak{}
\item
  Multi\allowbreak{}Cre\allowbreak{}dit\allowbreak{}Contr\allowbreak{}ibutor
  /
  Three\allowbreak{}Cre\allowbreak{}dit\allowbreak{}Contr\allowbreak{}ibutor
  mean at least two/three distinct credit records. Different roles
  provide witnesses; these class names do not prove exactly two/three
  unique roles across an entire career. More precise display names:
  Multi\allowbreak{}Cre\allowbreak{}dit\allowbreak{}Contr\allowbreak{}ibutor
  /
  Three\allowbreak{}Cre\allowbreak{}dit\allowbreak{}Contr\allowbreak{}ibutor.\allowbreak{}
\item
  Multi\allowbreak{}Gen\allowbreak{}re\allowbreak{}Film,
  Multi\allowbreak{}Awa\allowbreak{}rd\allowbreak{}Film and
  Frequent\allowbreak{}\allowbreak{}Producti\allowbreak{}on\allowbreak{}Compan\allowbreak{}y
  are not included as inferred classes: different
  genre/\allowbreak{}aw\allowbreak{}ard/\allowbreak{}film\allowbreak{}
  IRIs alone do not establish inequality. COUNT DISTINCT can answer a
  database\allowbreak{}-\allowbreak{}style question, but cannot be
  presented as OWL DL entailment.
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection{Reference scope}\label{reference-scope}

The official 2016-10 reference and official
extracti\allowbreak{}on-\allowbreak{}frame\allowbreak{}work development
vocabulary (790 classes) were checked. Semantic alternatives reviewed
include Actor, Movie\allowbreak{}Dir\allowbreak{}ector,
Screen\allowbreak{}Wr\allowbreak{}iter, Producer, Writer,
Artistic\allowbreak{}\allowbreak{}Genre,
Literary\allowbreak{}\allowbreak{}Genre, MovieGenre;
dbo:\allowbreak{}occu\allowbreak{}pation ranges over
Person\allowbreak{}Fu\allowbreak{}nction, while dbo:role and
artist\allowbreak{}Fu\allowbreak{}nction are strings and cannot replace
the local credit-\allowbreak{}t\allowbreak{}o-\allowbreak{}role entity
link. dbo:year (gYear) is generic, whereas
ex:\allowbreak{}relea\allowbreak{}se\allowbreak{}Year explicitly
represents an integer
earliest\allowbreak{}-\allowbreak{}release\allowbreak{} summary; it does
not claim equivalence to dbo:year or a complete releaseDate. The
archived reference hash is:
a0ca30c8\allowbreak{}f25cf2d2\allowbreak{}957639dc\allowbreak{}ad4fd111\allowbreak{}5f37a856\allowbreak{}d6d542fd\allowbreak{}50bba3bf\allowbreak{}9fa6bf9f\allowbreak{}.\allowbreak{}
The current official runtime, starring and MovieGenre pages were
cross-\allowbreak{}ch\allowbreak{}ecked.\allowbreak{} Absence in this
version is not proof that no equivalent vocabulary exists anywhere;
local
C\allowbreak{}R\allowbreak{}E\allowbreak{}A\allowbreak{}T\allowbreak{}E/\allowbreak{}\allowbreak{}E\allowbreak{}\allowbreak{}X\allowbreak{}T\allowbreak{}E\allowbreak{}N\allowbreak{}D
decisions are scoped to the checked DBpedia vocabulary and the stated
meanings.

\subsubsection{Target size}\label{target-size}

The overview uses 14 main concepts: dbo:Film, Person, Actor, Company,
Award, MovieGenre, Country, Language;
ex:\allowbreak{}\allowbreak{}Contr\allowbreak{}ibution,\allowbreak{}
Contribu\allowbreak{}tion\allowbreak{}Role\allowbreak{}, Filmmaker,
ActionFilm,
Award\allowbreak{}Win\allowbreak{}ning\allowbreak{}Film\allowbreak{} and
Multi\allowbreak{}Cre\allowbreak{}dit\allowbreak{}Contr\allowbreak{}ibutor.\allowbreak{}
Supporting hierarchy, provenance and inferred subsets are listed
separately. There are 10 domain inferred classes, including seven
additional classes requested in section 13, plus four supporting credit
subclasses and the reused Actor inference. This deliberately exceeds the
approximate 5--8 final target to satisfy the stronger requirement of
seven additional supported classes; no empty classes were added to reach
a count.

\subsection{11. Cardinality and validation
limitations}\label{cardinality-and-validation-limitations}

Exactly 1 constrains each Contribution endpoint and role. It does not
validate completeness under the open-world assumption: an absent filler
may exist without an explicit assertion. Structural checks must
separately enforce that the exported record contains its three fields.

At least 2 / at least 3 are genuine HermiT results on
has\allowbreak{}Contr\allowbreak{}ibution.\allowbreak{} Example:
Christopher Nolan has distinct directing, writing and producing credit
records; those records cannot collapse into one because hasRole is
functional and Director\allowbreak{}\allowbreak{}Role, WriterRole and
ProducerRole are distinct. No inequality is fabricated for people,
genres, awards, companies or films.

The negative Multi\allowbreak{}Gen\allowbreak{}re\allowbreak{}Film test
separately adds the requested min 2 genre definition and obtains zero DL
members. A production class is excluded because the dataset cannot prove
two genre fillers distinct. Future additions require
source-\allowbreak{}s\allowbreak{}upported\allowbreak{} inequality or a
justified identity model; a blanket AllDifferent on Wikidata IDs is not
justified by syntax.
